ELECTRONICS

For standard electronics and semiconductors, we maintain +15°C to +25°C Controlled Room Temperature throughout transit. For sensitive components like EV batteries or optical instruments, tighter ranges can be configured from manufacturer specifications.

Condensation occurs when cold electronics meet warm humid air. CBX maintains continuous controlled temperature to eliminate the differential that causes condensation. Climate Crates add an extra buffer during multi-leg transfers.
